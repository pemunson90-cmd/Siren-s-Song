# Photographic reconnaissance (core pillar, user-ratified 22 Oct 2026)

**Ratified premise:** Quietus is a vulnerable military recon submarine investigating the Sirens; photography is one of the most meaningful things the player does (Iron Lung influence). Rescue, industrial and fleet work support it and must not displace it. Full statement: top of docs/USER_DIRECTIVES.md. Everything under "PHOTO1 proposal" is a proposal; the premise is not.

## PRICED1 status (branch claude/priced1, one PR into claude/integration)
**Landed (focused tests, no human play):** a self-contained module prices each photograph. `simulation/photo_pricing/` holds finite film (Quietus 8, drone 4; every exposure that starts spends one, so unusable and interrupted ones cost film; none left means no exposure), the pure what-if readouts (reach rings for the key detail, flash reach, the three prices of light: flash, an 8 s still wait, closing in; "shutter heard to 520 m (760 m flashing), nearest known Siren in about N s"; live countdown for timed features), a briefing "photo problems" line, and a data pass on ops 0, 1, 3, 4, 5. `presentation/photo_pricing/` draws the viewfinder strip, the chart rings (clarity ring, Siren sight rings, heard ring) and picks four once-only hints (`priced_reach`, `priced_light`, `priced_window`, `priced_film`). Every tunable and every line of text is in `photo_pricing_table.gd`. Seams are listed in the header of `photo_pricing.gd`; reverting is removing the two folders and those hook lines. Save field `mission.photo_pricing` is optional and validated.
**Not done:** the pressure sleeve gates no bonus feature (no photo subject is band-guarded, and making one needs a band or detection rule); Siren sight 290/180 and alert speed 48 are mirrored in the table because simulation.gd holds them as literals; the "second clock" is the cycle window plus the live Siren estimate, with no separate patrol-gap readout; no human play of the new strip on a phone.

## PHOTO2 status (branch claude/photo2-images, one PR into claude/integration)
**Landed (focused tests plus local rendered screenshots, no human play):** the blocker is fixed: Siren studies live in `mission.photo.studies` (addressed at index 1000+), never in the objective list, so fixed-size operation checks and saves stay valid. The picture is drawn by `presentation/photo_scene.gd` from frame data: terrain profile, lamp-lit seabed, backscatter, marine snow, grain, the subject turned to its true aspect, features drawn only when resolved; shutter and a developing plate on filing and in the archive. Discovery: ops 0, 1, 3, 5, Prism Grave and Hammerwake chart unresolved targets only as an unknown area (lead), then an unnamed shape once a craft's camera sights it, and name them only when a frame resolves them (Furnace Drift and Silt Archive keep their earlier hidden form with an added area). Findings (`simulation/photo_findings.gd`): a resolved feature files a marked observation (route, hazard, weak point, cycle, person, Siren loop, gap between two studied loops, post and tether) that appears on the chart and in the archive viewer; film from Furnace Drift and Silt Archive marks the warm lane and scour gap on later charts. The compare text and dive grade no longer contradict frames.
**Not done:** op 4 (Well) keeps named targets; findings never alter combat, so most marks inform rather than change rules; late ops 6 to 13 and 23 to 29 still use photography only through Siren studies; no human or phone playtest of the new picture.

## PHOTO1 status (branch claude/photo1-recon, one PR into claude/integration)
**Landed (verified by focused scripted tests, not by play):** `simulation/photo_capture.gd` computes every frame from craft position, heading, optics, framing mode, light, water clarity, thermal shimmer, speed and the shared world clock. Subjects carry features with aspect arcs, detail needs and cycle windows; a shot stores a structured observation (conditions, craft, pose, resolved features with quality, a terrain depth profile) and several shots per target accumulate in the archive. Craft differ (Quietus: better lens and lamp, fixed arc, noisy; drones: wide gimbal, dark, must close in). Gear: OPTICS mount plus tele lens, flash (finite charges, louder), thermal imager, low-light back, all cut from archive intel (`campaign/photo_intel.gd`). Returned film becomes persistent intel that changes later builds (Last Boat Out middle gap from Silt Archive scour; Last Shift warm exhaust lane from Furnace Drift plume). Unnamed phenomena are found only by a craft's own forward camera (`discover`). Sirens can be studied (form, front array, observed path drawn on the chart). The camera panel is a live viewfinder drawn from frame data with WIDE/TIGHT, FLASH and FOCUS controls; the archive viewer shows stored frames with next and compare. Ops 0 to 5 use it; Cold Foundry gains an optional full-cycle base observation; Furnace Drift, Silt Archive and Prism Grave gain unnamed or optional photographic subjects. `work_sites.gd` now separates listen, photo and machine tasks.
**Closing audit (22 Oct):** all seven outcomes are covered by code and focused tests. Legacy tests that assumed an instant camera (Foundry scenario, recon consequences, Well depth journey) now stage a real vantage; the Well archive face now faces west so the quiet withdrawal route can photograph it. Core suite 794 checks, 0 failures. Proof ops: tutorial 0 to 5, Furnace Drift and Silt Archive unknowns (Glass Reach), Prism Grave facets, Iron Choir 25 warm lane from Furnace Drift film, Cold Foundry base cycle study.
**Not done / honest limits:** no human or touch-injected play of the new viewfinder; the Siren base study is one long observation, not a campaign arc; photography is MIDDLE band only (depth band affects light through mission depth, not a band choice); ops 6 to 13 and 22 to 29 use the system only through siren studies and intel effects; no annotation tool; panel layout on Fold7 cover is unmeasured; balance numbers are first-pass.

## Audit of the game as of 22 Oct (verified in code, not played)
**V7:** four fixed photo targets, instant CAPTURE within 72 of the nearest craft. Quality = prox-based, drone 0.55+0.45p, sub 0.25+0.45p. Costs 14 suspicion and +8 Hunt. Final A/B/C. The picture is a flash and a ring. No framing, heading or stored image.
**Godot now:** capture is a timed hold (MIDDLE band, within 240 m, clear line of sight, under 12 m/s, interruption resets; per-craft state). Quality is the V7 formula at 240 m and only graded for kind "" or "photo". The capture panel shows one of 7 authored PNGs chosen by objective, independent of where the craft is. Archive keeps best grade per record. A drone sampler takes 8 s audible field samples.
**Already useful:** per-craft physical exposure, drone vs sub constants, contacts and depth-band sight checks, Hunt floor and guard alert floors from secured records, evidence persistence, a research and facts path that can consume observations (campaign/glass_research.gd).

## Deficiencies, ranked
1. Quality is cosmetic: it reaches only the grade display. Nothing uses it for research, planning, unlocks or later ops.
2. The image does not depend on the photo: no heading, field of view, framing, light or conditions; distance only.
3. No discovery: targets are authored, charted and named in the brief; the art is identical at any grade.
4. Photography fades after op 5: ops 6 to 21 are essentially photo-free, ops 22 to 29 have none. Only op 0 is purely photographic; 1, 3, 5 mixed; 4 probably.
5. No camera equipment line (lens, flash, film, stabilizer); drone vs sub differs only by a constant.
6. Thin archive: one best shot per record, no comparison or annotation.

## Drift from the identity
Spec and parity ledger accepted "temporary" illustrations and a display-only grade ("every grade preserves completion"). Blackwater and Glass Reach reused the camera button for hydrophone "listening" reception, which also removed the drone/sub photo difference. Iron Choir work is entirely industrial and rescue. Rescue and machinery displaced reconnaissance exactly as the directive warns.

## PHOTO1 proposal (one sole builder, one branch, internal checkpoints, one PR)
Seams: new `simulation/photo_capture.gd` (heading, field of view, distance, band, light and thermal conditions, resulting frame with a visibility mask; replaces the hard 240 m radius); `Recon.secured` writes a structured observation (facts actually visible plus quality) instead of three scalars; grade becomes a function of the frame; equipment catalog gains camera gear (lens, flash, film or exposure limits) gated by upgrades; observed facts feed campaign consequences and research (route reveals, enemy roles, recipes); new archive viewer (multi-shot per target, compare, annotate); the camera panel renders from frame data instead of static PNGs; split the "listen" and "photo" labels in `campaign/encounters/work_sites.gd`; save validation updated.
Playable proof: rework representative ops across the campaign so photography matters in each band: a locate-and-photograph unknown in Glass Reach, intelligence from an earlier photo that changes an Iron Choir approach, a Siren factory or behavior study, and keep the tutorial ops 0 to 5 honest. Fair information throughout; no omniscient rewards. Not every op needs a photo task.
Checks: focused tests during construction, one full CI at the PR, then the cold review plays it and judges against Iron Lung.
