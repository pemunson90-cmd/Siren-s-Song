## Progression and discovery

Campaign completion unlocks further missions and preserves retries/replay. That is useful progression, but recovered knowledge rarely changes how the next dive is understood or played. Most objectives still share SIGNAL SPIRE / RELAY MOUTH / ARCHIVE NODE names, the first three optical views share a spire treatment, and non-Foundry later views share the foundry image. Only Foundry objectives carry distinct retained optical evidence. The finale's warning is currently an assertion after collection rather than a detail the player can recognize and explain.

Equipment selection is particularly opaque. An actual fresh operations capture shows `Equipment: ENDURANCE` and one cycling button, without effects or tradeoffs. Source establishes endurance +20 oxygen, disruption +1 charge, survey +1 uplink; all are available immediately. The extra disruption has a concrete use above. Endurance's extra oxygen has no demonstrated benefit in those runs: base oxygen remains at least 97.5% in the successful disruption variants. This is evidence about these plans, not permission to accelerate oxygen or punish tactical pause. Explain the choice and find opportunities where information/equipment decisions matter before adding an economy or upgrade tree.

The ending's refusal to provide a firing solution fits the investigation premise. Strengthen its evidence trail rather than add a branching morality menu. A player should be able to point to what they saw and say why the song means departure or warning.

## Atmosphere, visual/audio identity and phone comprehension

The current charcoal submarine, pointed red-lit Siren, basalt texture and ink/teal/cyan/amber palette belong together. Quietus retains the reference's elongated outline. A second serpentine class is an approved visual direction, not an immediate missing-feature requirement.

C01/C02 communicate a continuous seabed, channels with local shelter, a monumental industrial destination and different craft jobs in one physical scene. Split Channel and Foundry move toward that. The boxed, rotated Foundry facade is clear as an obstacle but still feels less integrated than the references. Improve one mission's composition and navigable meaning together, rather than add more texture everywhere.

Zoom relieves small craft but introduces attention costs: distant Quietus/threats can leave the view, world labels crop at edges, and the selected station's optical panel covers a substantial lower-left region. FIT and the fixed threat strip are good mitigations. Judge these as tradeoffs during actual planning; a cropped distant label alone is not a consequential regression.

## Single recommended next cycle: earn the final warning and return

**Scope: mission six and its necessary evidence/presentation, after current touch/native validation settles.** The player should use remote observations to understand the transmission, take Quietus through a reasoned exposure/cover decision, and bring a recognizable warning home. This consolidates the campaign's best systems and gives its ending practical meaning.

A concrete starting design within existing mechanics is two distinguishable remote observations of the transmission source and outbound vector, followed by a clearly explained shipborne decoding vantage and a covered return. Existing prerequisite, required-unit, timed acquisition, physical-cover and retained-evidence primitives can express this. The shipborne step needs an in-world reason visible in briefing/evidence—such as resolving a weak packet from a sheltered receiver position—rather than a bare unit restriction. Exact geometry should be authored from the actual camera/route experience. Keep the current warning/departure interpretation; no new enemy class or campaign economy is required.

The crucial constraint is **choice**, not forcing a prescribed drone performance. Preserve a slower solo-sub camera path and recovery after real drone loss. Drones should buy information, timing or shelter for Quietus, while a player can still improvise. A mandatory shipborne step would intentionally trade away the current remote-completion strategy; accept that only for a clearer, more involving finale, never merely to eliminate the measured 0m result. Retain the current strategy as a comparison and consider whether remote completion should remain a coherent alternate approach. Give the finale a different response/return rhythm from Foundry's camera-provoked carrier pulse; for example, decoding can reveal an existing opportunity to leave instead of spawning another punitive attack. Do not silently reveal hidden enemies as the reward.

Judge the cycle through a small connected set of actual evidence:

- Normal bounded observations must distinguish the array, the two evidence purposes, and the route/decoding decision. Retain enough recovered detail for the ending to refer to something inspectable. A SURVEY beauty frame alone is insufficient.
- Compare a plausible first approach with a considered covered/coordinated plan, and show a real interrupted-exposure or drone-loss recovery plus viable solo fallback. Record information available at decision time, sub movement, tools, drone outcomes and hull separately. A longer solver time or harsher damage is not the success criterion.
- Capture cover and unfolded planning, useful local zoom/FIT, acquisition, changed understanding, actual return and ending. Confirm touch camera movement remains presentation-only, pause remains unlimited, EXTRACT reliably overrides conditions, saves retain the warning evidence, and UI cues work muted. Use focused changed-behavior tests and existing regression coverage; do not inflate counts as a substitute for inspection.

Expected player value: a memorable capstone in which the player can explain both **what the Sirens were communicating** and **how their plan got Quietus home**. It should also expose the remaining contextual camera/loadout teaching needs in a concrete situation. Blackwater's relay identity, Listening Well's encounter identity, richer equipment progression and wider environment variety remain later opportunities; this report does not ratify them as finished or bundle them into the next cycle.
