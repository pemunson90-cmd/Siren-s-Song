## Confirmed new defect, corrected in follow-up: hidden queue mode

**Moderate impact; reproducible on both profiles.** On paused Shelf Signal, select Drone A → tap open water at (600,800) → toggle QUEUE → MORE ORDERS → CAMERA (out-of-range rejection) → tap (900,800). These world coordinates describe the retained replay, not screen tap positions.

At 6388113 the advanced list still shows `+ QUEUE ON`. At 6ce6cc0 the replacement secondary page hides that button while retaining `queue_mode=true`; CAMERA replaces the transient queue message with readiness/range text. The chart prompt remains generic `TAP ROUTE`. The final tap appends `(900,800)` and converts the first point to PASS, although no persistent visible element says that the next chart tap appends. This can extend a stale plan when the player intends to redirect a craft. Selecting another craft does clear queue, but the repro deliberately retains the same craft.

Independent actual GUI probes exit 0 after import on both exact sources and both profiles: [probe](../validation/production/density/fresh-review/queue_intent_probe.gd), `queue-{baseline,candidate}{,-inner}.log` in the [evidence directory](../validation/production/density/fresh-review/). The initial unimported scratch runs failed on missing imported textures; those failed attempts are retained and are fixture failures, not product defects.

Bounded remedy: persist selected craft and REPLACE/QUEUE intent on both pages, with the chart's next-tap meaning visible after any status message. Keep queue semantics and enlarged targets. Do not silently cancel an authored queue merely to conceal it.
