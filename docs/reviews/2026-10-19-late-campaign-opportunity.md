## One experiment: verify the Well's acoustic imprint

Prototype **optional versus required verification of a deliberate Quietus motion signature**, on new Well starts only. Begin with a small inspectable simulation/presentation experiment before commissioning a full asset set. Keep the current hunter, hearing threshold, sight/cover, lock, movement, stock and pause rules unchanged.

**Preferred optional variant:** the first recovered Well record presents a locally observed unknown pattern and an explicit opportunity to compare Quietus. The player can bring the archive home remotely with an honest “possible mimicry, unverified” interpretation, or risk a short **moving** acoustic pass to confirm it. A drone can observe while Quietus takes a chosen cruise route; solo Quietus can make its pass, brake under cover and inspect the return. Use the actual known own-motion emission condition, not a new sonar button or an arbitrary waiting cadence. A local return must become observable through ordinary sensors and retain a recognizable original comparison record. Confirmed evidence supports the engine-note debrief; an unverified result must not assert the same proof. Teach this choice before the last required record, because the current all-records/at-home rule can immediately end the remote dive.

This preserves the current remote win as a legitimate lower-exposure decision, while making further investigation a visible risk for better understanding. It is not an equipment reward, morality meter or new global progression economy. Potential loss: optional evidence may be skipped or read as inconsequential lore. If no meaningful plan changes, added caption panels alone do not deliver the intended gain.

**Required comparison variant:** make confirming the same moving signature part of one existing record's requirements. This intentionally removes current remote-only completion on fresh starts. It is justified only if choosing a pass, coordinating observation and recovering Quietus adds a clearer decision than the optional version; extra travel alone is insufficient. Do not turn it into another stationary shipborne receiver like the finale. Preserve old active Well saves' existing rules rather than migrating a mission underneath the player.

**Exact next implementation experiment:** compare baseline, optional and required versions with (1) the retained remote plan, (2) one coordinated covered acoustic pass, (3) an exposed/impatient pass retaining any real loss, and (4) slower solo/drone-loss recovery. Record the information visible when choosing, Quietus motion/noise, actual observed hunter response, tools, retained evidence and return separately. The local response may acknowledge our emission; it must not announce hidden enemy reception, guaranteed diversion or safety. Capture emission→observable return→interpretation plus an unattended continuation. Reject or revise the mandatory version if its only gain is forcing submarine displacement, and reject a timing gate that creates dead waiting. Promote a variant only after actual player-facing comparison; no sensing/speed/resource nerf to recover hull.

This proposes a causal investigation built from existing noise, route, sensor, observation and evidence primitives. Whether the Well's local response is represented as a waveform comparison or another original readable artifact is still a bounded design choice. It does not authorize a new audio-recognition subsystem or copying another game's instruments.
