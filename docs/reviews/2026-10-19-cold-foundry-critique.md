## What the evidence supports

**The factory now belongs to the navigation problem.** The western shelf, central basalt spine, southern passage and far-side recovery buoy give Quietus an intelligible transit while drones work. The drawn factory perimeter shares `factory_rect`/`structures` with movement blocking and LOS. Both `blocked()` and `line_clear()` use that structure. The new physical-footprint check passes; ordinary routes reach the vantages and extraction. I found no supported movement/LOS disagreement in this candidate. Initial chart geography is explicitly chart memory, not proof of passive omniscience.

**The records are distinct discoveries rather than a repeated counter.** `furnace.png` and `archive_furnace.png` show a bright circular casting process; `cradle.png` and `archive_cradle.png` show elongated hulls, open frames and an outbound gap. Their names, images and retained captions support the construction/departure interpretation in `debrief.png`. Furnace-before-cradle is explicitly explained by the casting-pattern prerequisite, and either drone or Quietus can acquire them. These are separately authored optical illustrations, not a live camera rendering of the tactical factory. That is a presentational abstraction, not evidence of a false gameplay capture. Human recognition of the implication before reading the caption remains untested.

**Exposure has understandable consequences.** The briefing warns that the industrial perimeter is exposed and cameras may provoke a carrier response. At video 1s, the player sees the active camera progress, named drone targets and beam countdowns. At 4s Drone A is lost and Quietus is targeted; at 7s firing has reduced hull to 79%. The second record produces an actual eight-second noise source at the cradle. Existing patrol hearing/occlusion processes it; no enemy spawn or hidden direct target assignment is required. The expanding response ring, footer and dedicated factory audio event give a visual counterpart when muted. The captured unfolding is consequential without establishing that a novice can yet parse every label.

**Recovery is fair in the tested situations.** The unprotected exposed solo plan loses at 32.9s after one record; the finite protective solo plan wins at 55.6s/100% hull with three disruptions. The staged protective plan wins at 34.2s/100%, both drones alive, with three disruptions and one decoy. Neither timing nor clean final hull alone measures experiential quality. More usefully, the additional delayed-reaction probe below demonstrates an actual drone loss followed by intact extraction. The ordinary full-campaign fallback also wins Cold Foundry with both drones initially unavailable at 50.6s/100%. Do not call losing a drone an automatic mission failure or demand an arbitrary drone gate.

**Drones already support divided attention and exposure delegation.** Quietus can follow its southern route while a drone holds a risky optical vantage. The delayed-reaction experiment exchanges Drone A for a saved disruption charge. This is more than a faster identical submarine route, although no persistent loss cost or deeper campaign equipment consequence is demonstrated here. Existing unlimited pause, autonomous exposure, station task labels and reliable route replacement support deliberate planning; do not replace them with frantic input pressure.

## Largest remaining gaps

1. **Verified visual obstruction: encounter labels compete at the cradle.** In `cradle.png`, `TARGET · DRONE B` and its countdown occupy the departure-marker region. In `inner-response.png`, craft names, response text and extraction cluster around Quietus/Drone B. Video 4–7s is worse: target, recovered, extraction, response and firing/mode labels compete during the decision. The newest source anchors `CARRIER RESPONSE · FROM CRADLE` inside the factory rather than the older `ACOUSTIC ORIGIN` positioning visible in the unfolded/live captures; `response.png` shows the improved placement. That improves one label but does not resolve the other overlaps. Reproduce by running `capture_foundry.gd -- --stage=cradle` or `response_live` at the documented cover/unfolded sizes. Low-cost proposal: reserve a clear location for the current target/countdown, displace nearby labels, and quiet recovered-objective labels during active danger. Preserve source/target identity and stale-contact distinction.

2. **Verified scale/comprehension limitation, with physical comfort still unknown.** Both form factors retain the entire 1600×960 world; there is currently no zoom/pan. Small dark craft and narrow passages require labels to carry their identity, while many equally weighted buttons remain visible. The unfolded layout successfully puts controls below the map, and the pointer/layout checks pass; neither proves friendly thumb interaction or that a newcomer understands IF CONTACT / WAIT DRONE. The user's requested zoom/pan and touch cycle directly addresses this highest remaining opportunity. Validate selection, route placement, pause, camera and escape at useful local scales, with unobtrusive contextual order feedback. Do not treat logical 48px button height as physical-device comfort evidence.

3. **Bounded tactical finding: the late decoy in the winning script is redundant in this comparison.** Removing only that decoy produces the same 34.2s/100% return with both drones alive and zero EMP left. The three earlier disruptions still cover extraction. Thus the canonical trace establishes a victory, not a causal diversion benefit. This is not proof that decoys are generally ineffective; Split Channel's existing controlled return demonstrates a benefit elsewhere. Low-cost correction is to label this Foundry decoy unproven/redundant and simplify any teaching trace. Before adding enemies or consuming more tools, compare an observable diversion/cover choice if further Foundry tactical depth is sought. A harder solver is not the objective.

4. **Aesthetic opportunity: the tactical factory still reads as a bounded upright sprite.** It now has a coherent solid footprint, but the thin rectangular outline and isolated vertical machine are less integrated and monumental than C01/C02's continuous industrial canyon edge. Optical records carry much of the alien scale and richness. Quietus retains the selected elongated, low silhouette; the pointed ray retains the approved strange morphology. Those identities should be preserved. After touch clarity, a more purposeful relationship between facade, perimeter and nearby geology would add more than extra surface detail. This is a visual judgment, not a collision defect or a demand to reproduce a concept painting literally.

## Exact additional probes

Temporary workspace script: `/tmp/sirens_foundry_review.gd`, run with `scripts/toolchain/headless.sh --script /tmp/sirens_foundry_review.gd`. It imports the unchanged production `foundry_command_plan.gd` as `Plan`; all behavior below uses its ordinary command helpers. No units, captures, enemy states or victories are forced.

**Delayed recovery:** `s=Plan.play(true,"cradle",true)`; set `s.paused=false`; `Plan.advance(s,delay,false)`; then `Plan.travel(s,0,s.mission.extraction,true)`.
