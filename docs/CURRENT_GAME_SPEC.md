# Siren's Song — current intended game specification
**Status:** Active living production specification · adopted 2026-10-19
**Supersedes for current design:** historical `SIRENS_SONG_functional_spec.md`, pressure-design notes and single-level prototype briefs. Originals remain immutable as provenance and executable historical references, especially playable v7. The current user, this living spec, current design bible and ratified concept references govern interpretation; see SOURCE_AUTHORITY.

## Product ambition and never-ending Gauntlet
Build an original, Android-first 2D tactical ocean-stealth and reconnaissance game with cohesive, polished, deeply satisfying **AAA-caliber content and execution** as a directional quality ambition. Benchmark actual player experience, craftsmanship, interaction quality, art, sound, campaign pacing, enemy intelligence, systems integration, performance and accessibility against excellent contemporary games. “Publishable” means content and execution quality; **store submission, monetization, marketing, legal paperwork, publisher accounts and other release administration are out of scope unless the user requests them**. No feature count, test count, APK export, six-level completion, release-candidate label, critic approval or self-declared quality rating ends the Gauntlet. The user alone may stop it.

Each iteration must identify the most important remaining *player-facing quality or depth improvement*, implement it, assess the experience against the previous playable baseline and against external exemplars as appropriate, reconcile regressions, and find the next substantive gain. Criticism can find major missing dimensions, redesign stale patterns and question prior solutions; avoid endlessly polishing small UI details while the game lacks compelling choices. A change with more code or tests but no meaningful player improvement is not a successful Gauntlet advance. Never impose an arbitrary maximum quality ceiling, an automatic completion threshold, or endless unproductive feature accumulation.

## Core fantasy and play loop
One vulnerable human pilot operates USS Quietus and two independently commanded drones against superior alien Sirens in an alien industrial ocean. **Perceiving risks exposure; speed creates acoustic risk; controlling several craft taxes the pilot's attention.** Real-time continuous simulation with unlimited tactical pause supports deliberate multi-step planning, including queued waypoint routes, observed-contact conditions, and extraction. The chart reveals only earned knowledge. Physical geography, currents, thermal gradients, sound propagation, sensors, and alien attention should produce readable, manipulable uncertainty.

Typical loop: brief and choose tools; pause and plan unit routes; move and listen; gather camera, sonar or hydrophone evidence; withstand or misdirect attention; adapt to discoveries; bring Quietus home; interpret the record and unlock distinct further opportunities. Events continue when unpaused; pause grants time to plan, never hidden information.

## Movement and tactical orders — next core milestone
- Preserve inertia, turning, safe pathfinding, physical clearance, explicit REPLACE/QUEUE, PASS/STOP waypoints, emergency route replacement, conditional hold, and sub/drone simultaneous routes.
- Enable **per-waypoint/segment propulsion programming**, e.g. cruise to A, switch silent after A, stop at B, resume cruise after B. Timing follows acceleration/deceleration physics, not instantaneous speed jumps. Support editing, insertion, deletion, and undo of individual planned waypoints with clear previews and persistent indication of selected craft/route intent.
- Keep route commands easy under unlimited tactical pause. Reduce accidental world orders, hidden modes and command-page hunting. Intentional divided-attention strain belongs in gameplay; clumsy mobile gestures and menus do not.
- Bring back useful v7 current fields and tactical thermal masking after direct code-verified adaptation; physics/terrain and acoustic consequences must be legible and compatible with Godot navigation.

## Acoustic perception and drones — next core milestone
- Replace oversimplified threshold-only motion hearing with readable **speed-dependent acoustic signature**, informed by playable v7 and the current Godot physics. Display truthful footprint/uncertainty as practical chart information.
- Basalt/structures attenuate, reflect, diffract and route underwater sound. Sound must not pass through rock as though it were empty water. Design a computationally practical, deterministic/seeded multi-path approximation allowing ambiguous arrivals around obstacles: Sirens infer plausible directions and sometimes search an incorrect branch. Do not apply arbitrary 50/50 decisions independent of geometry and signal strength. Separate acoustic sensing from visual occlusion; establish authored/tested edge cases and gameplay feedback.
- Quietus offers endurance, lower-signature operations and specialized hydrophone/mission duties; drones offer speed, independent information gathering and expendability but finite batteries and situation-specific stealth/equipment vulnerabilities. Research Duskers for specialized tools, limited information and meaningful risk without copying its interface or economy. Inspect v7's actual drone targeting/detection prior to changing Godot behavior. Preserve fallback sub reconnaissance when drones are lost.
- Preserve fair enemy perception: no omniscient sensor queries, fresh versus remembered contacts, local suspicion, legitimate investigation/search/return, and counterplay using cover and disruption.

## Siren threats and defensive tools
- Preserve three functioning roles: patrollers, station-bound sentinels and fast hunters; beam acquisition must be visibly telegraphed, interrupted by lost sight/stun, and obey actual terrain. Their combat arsenal can remain beam-centric while core systems are improved. More enemy combat tactics and weapon variety are future research, not a near-term requirement.
- **Restore v7's playable single-target knockdown torpedo**. Original JS provides 2 charges, target tap/selection, proximity check, roughly 150 *prototype-scale* range, 8-second pinned immobilization, threat/Hunt costs, feedback/sound, and invalid-target rejection. Its bola/down-thrust description is historical functional-spec provenance. Translate range/balance to Godot world scale through play; retain distinct tactical identity versus radial EMP (current Godot 340 world-unit radius, 12-second disruption). Verify direct code and tests rather than treating the old written “unbuilt” status as authoritative over playable v7.
- Preserve decoys, limited EMP, active sonar and survey, meaningful finite supplies and audio/visual enemy reaction. Evaluate v7 Hunt/suspicion interaction: discoveries should change the cost of extraction. Integrate or deliberately replace its global pressure floor through comparative play, never blindly copy numeric scales.

## Reconnaissance, evidence and progression
- Six currently authored missions: Shelf Signal (introduce stealth/recovery); Split Channel (multi-unit relay/uplink); Blackwater Relay (quiet request/reply versus audible sonar-assisted sampling); Cold Foundry (industrial reconnaissance/guard/extraction); Listening Well (hunter and optional signature comparison); Siren's Song (convergent final evidence, hydrophone decoding and extraction).
- These are an **initial campaign baseline**, not an upper bound on content or ambition. Each mission must force meaningfully different decisions, interactions, terrain affordances, discoveries, and escalating understanding; difficulties should progress chiefly through learnable tactical complexity and consequences, supported by fair density/resource tuning and novice tests. Reassess length, variety, pacing and post-six-mission expansion in every relevant quality pass.
- Current authored evidence illustrations and live optical acquisition are acceptable interim photography. Real viewpoint-dependent snapshots/framing and v7 proximity/drone photographic quality scoring are polish/content candidates, explicitly tracked rather than silently discarded.
- Preserve original, increasingly consequential discoveries and narrative coherence; optional records should teach and affect planning. Preserve camera and archive behavior already improved in Godot. Save/retry/autosave/backup and compatibility are required; independent profiles, copy/clear and export/import become a milestone when progression breadth warrants them.
- Retain v7 implemented environmental currents, thermal masking, action/Hunt pressure, narrative logs, acoustic footprint, speed-based hearing, targetable knockdown and photo grade in the parity ledger. Implement or explicitly decide alternative treatment based on player gain.

## Native input, visual and audio quality
- Mobile-first readable chart with touch pan/pinch/FIT and reliable taps, explicit mode/selection, forgiving targets, context-sensitive controls, route inspection/editing and no accidental orders. Support Fold7 cover and inner aspect ratios, while preserving real phone ergonomics. Critique actual interactions using useful established tactics/mobile-game patterns and user pain points; fixed geometry tests do not establish ease of use.
- Art: layered abyssal basalt, physically coherent cover, cyan/amber industrial structures, charcoal Quietus, angular ray/serpentine red-lit Sirens, legible sensor and evidence distinction. Continue visual exploration beyond generic UI polish.
- Audio: meaningful directional/semantic cues for propulsion, sonar, pressure, threats, loss, equipment and extraction, silence and dynamic contrast. User will personally judge subjective quality. Preserve mute/suspension correctness.

## Development governance and evidence
Dedicated independently mergeable branches/worktrees, one assigned integration reviewer, reconciliation against latest production, targeted tests at normal merge, full regression/native Android at meaningful milestones or native-specific risk. Preserve known-good baseline and original imported materials. Refresh AGENTS/CONTINUATION/WORKBENCH at task boundaries. Document accepted quality gains and costly tradeoffs; commission deeper independent criticism periodically for substantive playable experiences, not ritual certification of tiny deltas.

Current spec is revisable by new user direction and evidence-driven Gauntlet improvements. Each such improvement must be integrated into this living spec with source/rationale. No frozen exhaustive checklist can substitute for continued qualitative reevaluation.
