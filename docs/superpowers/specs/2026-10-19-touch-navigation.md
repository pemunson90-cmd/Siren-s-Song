# Touch navigation proposal — queued after Cold Foundry validation

Authority: user's explicit 19 October direction: production need not retain prototype UI/screen dimensions; zoom in/out and a usable, friendly touch UI are key features. This proposal refines implementation within that direction, subject to actual cover/unfolded evidence. Source hierarchy and the persistent Gauntlet remain binding. Do not start the major implementation cycle before the current candidate's independent/native assessment and deferred process checkpoint are secured.

Player problem: craft and danger are small in the whole-map overview, labels collide near objectives, and a tap-only chart makes closer inspection impossible. Retain an overview for planning, allow closer inspection and movement across the chart, and make route gestures distinguishable from navigation gestures. Screen dimensions are not a design constraint.

Proposed bounded challenger:

- Separate a presentation-only chart camera from simulation state. Whole-map fit remains initial/default recovery view. Pinch or +/- zoom around an anchor; drag pans; FIT restores the overview. Zoom/pan never advances simulation, changes sensor range or reveals hidden contacts. Keep pause, station selection, route intent and emergency replacement accessible.
- Tap release issues a route only after a single short stationary touch. Crossing a drag threshold or introducing a second finger cancels the pending order for the whole gesture. Continue tracking releases outside the map; cancel on menu, lifecycle or layout changes. Ignore touch-emulated duplicate mouse events on the chart while preserving native button input.
- Use one world↔screen transform for drawing, touch commands, camera overlays and native diagnostic target mapping. Clamp useful pan/zoom limits, avoid losing the chart, and keep overview recovery discoverable. Desktop wheel/drag and actual touch use the same camera rules.
- Inspect and improve action hierarchy on both Fold7 surfaces: primary actions and state first, advanced conditions/tool choices secondary where that reduces crowding. Do not expand a wall of buttons. Preserve explicit replace/queue and STOP/PASS feedback; do not hide emergency extraction or introduce modal targeting residue.
- Zoomed chart should improve craft/route inspection, with labels placed or abbreviated to avoid the Foundry cradle/threat/extraction collision. Preserve physical terrain/LOS and station-owned exposure behavior. Do not substitute a new background for the interactive world.

Evidence gates: failing gesture/transform assertions before implementation; actual synthetic touch sequences for pinch/drag/release/outside/cancel and tap after transformed view; zero orders from pan/pinch; pause invariance; ordinary all-mission and campaign-save checks remain green. Render cover/unfolded overview, close threat/optical route, panned view and recovery FIT. Native ordinary mission completion must still pass, using target coordinates in the full-window logical space. Native +/- touch smoke can supplement; physical Fold7 fingers/folding and human comfort remain a separate gate.

Promotion should compare the strongest verified baseline and challenger against concrete gains/tradeoffs, retain rejected gesture experiments and independently hunt confirmed regressions after deterministic checks. A fresh whole-game review is periodic, proportionate and may prioritize a different opportunity after the user's requested touch direction is met. No approval gate for routine implementation choices; user direction remains authoritative.
