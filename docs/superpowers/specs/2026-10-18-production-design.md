# Siren's Song production design

The binding production spec is [the architecture and creative contract](../../architecture/GAUNTLET.md), read with the immutable [design bible](../../../design/SIRENS_SONG_DESIGN_BIBLE_GAUNTLET_V1.md) and [source authority](../../SOURCE_AUTHORITY.md).

The user explicitly authorizes autonomous design, engine selection, implementation, independent critique and iteration. Routine choices do not need an additional approval round. Escalate changes to the premise or player role, material cost commitments, or unavailable environment/credential capabilities. Selected execution: Sol 6.1 production with fresh-context Sol criticism. Preserve the existing model policy.

Acceptance is evidence-driven: a complete six-mission campaign with progression and conclusion; fair acoustic/occluded tactical stealth; independent drones; clear touch planning; atmospheric rendered world and functional sound; versioned saves and lifecycle handling; installable Android artifact; reproducible scenarios and independent review. Every incomplete device/quality gate must remain visible in the workbench. Initial runnable milestones are not the finished game.
