# Source authority and provenance

The project uses an explicit distinction between Pat's decisions, ratified directions, tested observations, assistant proposals, and unapproved exploration.

## Authority hierarchy

1. Pat's current explicit decisions and approvals.
2. Current design bible and accepted amendments.
3. Approved concept selection ledger and recent visual review decisions.
4. Verified playtest findings and reproducible behavior.
5. Original conversation provenance and historical functional specifications.
6. Exploratory concept images, older prototypes, and assistant proposals.

Read its actual behavior and compare production against it before replacing or omitting mechanics; record retained, changed and missing behavior with rationale. Current explicit production decisions still govern; the historical wrapper/single-level/approval rules do not reactivate. [Addenda A and B](source-import/original-prototype-handoff/RATIFICATION.md) are now user-ratified directions despite B's preserved original “Proposal” label. Their source assertions still require honest verification; ratification does not make every historical inference a measured fact.

A newer proposal does not silently supersede an established decision. When documents disagree, record the discrepancy and seek a decision only if it materially affects the game's identity or production.

## Visual guidance

Use candidate gameplay references for scene composition and world language, the recent Quietus exterior for silhouette continuity, and the review PDF for known image-generation failure modes. Concept-art engineering cutaways are exploratory. Keep physical terrain, vehicles, contacts, and route/sensor overlays legible and distinguishable on mobile.

## Historical versus current scope

The 2026-06 prototype specifications constrained development to a single hand-authored level. Current production intent is a complete multi-level Android game with creative latitude for progression, mission design, environmental variation, and procedural asset techniques.
