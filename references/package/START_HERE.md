## Apply the references

- Carry top-down physical seabed, real obstacles, concealment and readable routes into the production 2D Android game. Cinematic perspective illustrates atmosphere and scale; the bible establishes gameplay camera/mode.
- Preserve Quietus’s recognizable reference silhouette, compact proportions, tapered bow, low dorsal profile, forward apertures and central aft screw. Internal cutaways and printed engineering figures remain exploratory.
- Favor the newer sleek Siren studies when earlier scene enemies are bulky. Their role/size taxonomy, articulation and detailed mechanics remain creative territory.
- Preserve cyan/turquoise and amber brutalist alien industry, underwater light attenuation, rock and trench mass, separate sensor overlays, and independently tasked drones.
- An attacked drone is the shared comparative art encounter. Design the full range of missions and stealth situations authorized by the bible.
- Treat rendered route geometry and camera cones as illustrative. Implement collision, occlusion, known information and touch usability from actual game rules.
- Preserve provenance: likely candidates are provisional; assistant observations are assessments; generated fittings and labels do not become ratified specifications.
