# SIREN'S SONG — Visual Exploration Plan v3

**Date:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** Planning complete for review; image generation requires Pat's explicit approval.  
**Purpose:** Portable handoff for six separate concept-art experiments for the Android-first, 2D top-down tactical submarine stealth game *Siren's Song*.

## Project and scene context

The player pilots the experimental **USS Quietus**, a believable but interesting single-person US military exploration/reconnaissance submarine. The premise draws on underwater UFO-base reports and speculation. The mission involves navigating an alien-controlled deep-sea environment, photographing evidence of a factory, and surviving technologically superior Sirens. The game emphasizes stealth, reconnaissance, acoustic detection, tactical pause, independently commanded drones, route plotting, terrain occlusion, and inertia.

**Visual priority:** The actual world is a stylized-realistic, physically depicted **2D underwater seabed seen top-down**. The prototype's sparse geometric sonar visualization was a development expedient, not the intended final environment. Sensor readings and route overlays are separate information layers from the actual mass and appearance of vehicles, rocks, and factory structures. Concept paintings may use elevated three-quarter views to explore the same world.

## Agreed direction

- **Art:** Grounded painterly sci-fi, stylized realism and strong silhouettes, cinematic lighting, credible military engineering. **Vary rendering treatment across all six generations.**
- **Camera:** Explore near-vertical top-down, elevated three-quarter, and wide environmental establishing views.
- **Seabed:** Ancient eroded cliffs, volcanic basalt, jagged rocks, deep trenches, underwater canyons, and terrain altered by alien construction. Large rocks are genuine navigation and concealment obstacles.
- **Quietus:** Single-person experimental US naval craft, compact pressure hull integrated with blended hydrodynamic surfaces and unusual but credible propulsion. Draw inspiration from real experimental American submersibles and grounded science fiction. Interesting, restrained silhouette; avoid gratuitous busyness. **Vary the Quietus design and prominence between paintings.**
- **Sirens:** Physically substantial, angular, dark alien craft with selective red luminous features; **vary configuration, scale, and role**, from drone-sized hunters through interceptors to enormous heavy craft. Their sensor contacts are distinct from their physical forms. Quietus is larger than small hunter Sirens and smaller than heavy classes.
- **Factory:** **Brutalist alien industrial architecture**, monolithic slabs, geometric excavations, recessed manufacturing cavities, sprawling machinery, structures embedded in seabed. Cyan/turquoise illumination and warm amber/molten-orange manufacturing activity; color may indicate different processes. Vary prominence from distant mystery to overwhelming complex.
- **Lighting:** Vary from legible cinematic blue-green seabed to near-black abyss with local vehicle lights, red hostile accents, suspended sediment and alien industrial glow.
- **Tactical overlay:** Thin plotted movement routes and waypoints, sensor contacts, and target indicators; readable, subordinate to the physical world. Do not turn the concept paintings into instrument-only prototype screenshots.
- **Action:** One Siren fires a superheated disintegration ray at **Drone A**; **Drone B** independently photographs the encounter or factory. Explore different moments and physical weapon effects across images: imminent impact, thermal rupture, cutting, melting, fragmentation, disintegration.
- **Output:** Multiple **separate** image generations, not a collage; vary format, art style, composition, environment visibility, Quietus prominence/design, and Siren configurations. Preserve the common gameplay encounter so comparisons are useful.

## Six generation briefs

### 01 — Into the Gauntlet
**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Style:** Painterly stylized realism. **Camera:** Nearly straight top-down, gameplay-adjacent. **Visibility:** High.

A medium-prominence Quietus with a broad blended experimental hull navigates a readable, richly textured seabed. A thin plotted route curls between towering eroded rocks and trenches. A small faceted hunter Siren, roughly one-third the Quietus's size, fires a focused red-orange thermal beam into Drone A, producing localized melting. Drone B photographs the factory from a safer position. The factory is a distant partially concealed brutalist landmark. Route curves, waypoints, sensor contacts and a target marker are visible.

**Evaluate:** Top-down world readability, beautiful playable-looking terrain, clean silhouettes, credible tactical geometry.

### 02 — Contact
**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Style:** Cinematic military realism. **Camera:** Elevated three-quarter. **Visibility:** Medium.

A prominent compact Quietus emerges between imposing submerged cliffs, with credible pressure-hull engineering, subtle US naval markings, and experimental split propulsion. An asymmetric, angular medium interceptor Siren fires a concentrated ray that cuts Drone A into incandescent sections. Drone B records from behind a rock projection. Suspended sediment catches the beam's light. The factory appears in the far distance as colossal brutalist masses with cyan lighting.

**Evaluate:** Physical vehicle credibility, dramatic composition, believable lighting, readable cause-and-effect.

### 03 — The Factory Below
**Format:** 21:9 ultrawide. **Style:** Monumental architectural concept painting. **Camera:** Wide elevated establishing view. **Visibility:** Low, with factory light providing depth.

The Quietus is tiny against an immense trench. The sprawling brutalist factory occupies much of the far canyon wall and floor: monolithic slabs, geometric excavation, deep machinery bays, cyan/turquoise structural lighting and amber molten manufacturing regions. Include a small hunter Siren attacking Drone A in the distance, and a much larger heavy Siren patrolling near the installation. Drone B photographs the complex. The weapon strike is a tiny but sharp hot accent.

**Evaluate:** Scale, industrial architecture, unfamiliar construction logic, contrasting Siren classes.

### 04 — Blackwater Passage
**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Style:** Atmospheric dark science-fiction painting. **Camera:** Oblique top-down. **Visibility:** Very low and patchy.

An experimental flattened Quietus with unconventional propulsion skirts basalt ridges, mineral plumes and fissures. Alien brutalist geometry intrudes into the volcanic geology. A compact triangular Siren with separated angular appendages and narrow red-lit seams emerges from concealment, striking Drone A; the drone begins breaking into glowing particulate debris. Drone B photographs from behind cover. Only faint factory edges and industrial lights penetrate the murk. The planned route bends around actual occluding rock.

**Evaluate:** Stealth mood, terrain obstruction, selective lighting, coherent ambush geography.

### 05 — Descent to the Signal
**Format:** 9:16 portrait. **Style:** Premium stylized mobile-game illustration. **Camera:** Elevated top-down composition. **Visibility:** Medium-high.

A prominent streamlined Quietus with swept blended surfaces and recessed propulsion occupies the lower region. A plotted route winds through a long rocky passage toward a distant brutalist factory with turquoise and amber industrial light. A broad, flattened medium hunter Siren attacks Drone A farther along the route. Drone B photographs from a lateral rock recess. Use confident simplified silhouettes, detailed environmental texture and clear visual hierarchy suitable for a tall Android screen.

**Evaluate:** Portrait legibility, route direction, separation of the two drones, appealing mobile-game identity.

### 06 — Disintegration
**Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Style:** High-detail hard-surface sci-fi illustration. **Camera:** Closer elevated three-quarter. **Visibility:** Dramatic localized illumination.

A heavy angular Siren interceptor, significantly larger than the Quietus, dominates the encounter. Its armored asymmetrical volumes, recessed weapon aperture and red light features read as engineered physical structures. A sustained superheated beam breaks Drone A into luminous fragments, vapor and particulate debris. The Quietus, a narrower experimental blended-hull variation, maneuvers behind a rock barrier; Drone B photographs from concealment. The distant factory remains recognizable as a brutalist industrial silhouette.

**Evaluate:** Alien craft morphology, weapon origin and physical impact, restrained high-detail engineering.

## Image generation and review procedure

1. **Wait for explicit approval** before generating any image.
2. Generate each painting as its own image. Begin with **01 Into the Gauntlet** and **02 Contact** to contrast gameplay-adjacent readability against cinematic depiction, unless Pat directs another order.
3. Retain distinct designs across the six: the exploration is deliberately comparative. Do not prematurely converge on one Quietus, Siren, or rendering style.
4. Review results for (a) coherent spatial navigation around real obstacles; (b) readable Quietus and distinct Siren silhouettes; (c) separate drone roles; (d) visible source, path and effect of weapon beam; (e) brutalist factory identity; (f) credible underwater light, materials, and scale; (g) legible but subordinate tactical overlays.
5. Combine successful features only after review. Follow-up vehicle sheets, factory studies, and gameplay mockups may be developed from the chosen directions.
