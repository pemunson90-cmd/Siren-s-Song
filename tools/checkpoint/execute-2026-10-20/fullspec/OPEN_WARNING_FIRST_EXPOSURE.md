## Result and judgement

I completed The Open Warning by receiving the local acknowledgement, manually opening the service gate, deliberately dispatching the recipient along the sheltered course, observing its physical transit, and placing Quietus in the northeast recovery basin until arrival was received. The ending was **SURFACE RECKONING**, with **RECIPIENT AT REFUGE · SHELTERED COURSE** and **THIS CROSSING · ARRIVAL RECEIVED** (frame `1792507536773364511`). Recipient health was 100%. Quietus remained at hull 100 in every resource display I inspected. I used one disruption pulse. Both drones were left at deployment and the ending explicitly recorded **OUTSIDE RECOVERY**, with bare replacements available (`1792507656434077803`): successful warning, actual fleet recovery loss.

The physical send, hunter reaction, local acknowledgement, gate and moving recipient form a coherent playable operation. The strongest moment was the hunter pursuing Quietus after the second broadcast and the decision to spend disruption before a beam resolved. The weakest consequential experience was the final protected transit: after choosing the gate route, I moved Quietus ahead and waited through the journey without another protective decision. The ending recognized the course, but most of the promised human and political stakes existed in prose. Finishability is demonstrated for this policy and this fixture; compelling escort play is not established.

The crossing earned a basic mechanical success: I physically prepared the route, deliberately released the recipient and waited in the right place to receive its arrival. It did not earn the scale of the dramatic resolution through the crossing itself. SHELTERED attracted me because I had ample oxygen, no observed gate-side threat and no desire to risk the recipient in the eastern hunter's watch. Its observed costs were positioning, an audible release, oxygen spent moving and interface work; no gate-side attack or scarce resource sacrifice occurred. EXPOSED initially had some appeal as the briefing's direct, ungated route, then became still less attractive when recipient STATUS described it as both longer and exposed. I never tested whether its real course was rewarding. Unused drones/tools were unnecessary for **my completed sheltered policy**, not proven unnecessary for the operation's other strategies.

## What worked, and its limits

- The game starts paused; unlimited pause let me inspect and recover from interface confusion without tactical punishment. This is valuable for a complex touch tactics game.
- Sending from Quietus's actual position, then seeing a hunter investigate and pursue, made acoustic exposure concrete. The beam countdown and finite disruption gave me one legible, consequential response. No source knowledge was needed.
- Acknowledgement required local presence and a timed listen. The recipient did not depart until explicitly instructed, physically crossed the map, and the ending required actual arrival. These are observed gains over a purely declarative objective, but do not by themselves establish varied or compelling decisions.
- The tailored sheltered-course ending and drone recovery entries recognized actual choices. Their presentation and advance salience need development.

Expected comparison standards come from the design strengths of Door Kickers (route planning and reliable target selection), Mark of the Ninja (danger information linked to choices), Duskers (drone utility and irreversible scarcity), and Barotrauma (credible acoustic tension and crew stakes). **No blind side-by-side footage comparison was performed in this task.** These are reference bars, not measured superiority claims. Dummy audio prevents a substantive sound judgement.
