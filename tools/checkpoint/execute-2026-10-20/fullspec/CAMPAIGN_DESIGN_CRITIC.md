## Material findings

### D1 — Overt finales skip the action that earns their political and humanitarian payoff

**Evidence / confidence:** High, source-observed baseline. `resolution_missions.gd:66–71` requires a warning from chosen position, acoustic reception at an anonymous fixed reply source and local decode. `resolution_operations.gd:25–29` activates that source only if the outgoing wave has a supported path. This is a real earned reception event. However the source evidence explicitly says identity, intent and wider chain remain uncertain, and no recipient action, prior relationship or evacuation progress is a condition. `endings.gd:4,6,8–15` then asserts fleet restraint, human escorts/Siren carrier cooperation, or a fleet entirely saved and thousands rescued. Resolver checks only thread, final route and operation completion.

**Player consequence:** Choosing an emission origin and surviving extraction does not make the player feel responsible for the pact/disaster outcome. The finale jumps from radio task to world resolution, weakening the preceding concern about evidence versus firing solutions.

**Release severity / class:** High narrative payoff weakness; **Expansion**, not progress-blocker. The active ID13 work is directly relevant and must be reviewed after integration, not assumed to cure the whole issue beforehand.

**Expected quality:** An authored epilogue may legitimately know more than the pilot. Every civilian need not be simulated and no universal uncertainty disclaimer is required. The player's consequential action nevertheless needs a credible authored causal bridge to the specific ending: establish who can act, what they need, and what the chosen warning changes.

**Substantive improvement:** Make a recipient/route response materially happen because of the warning or prior earned trust, then let the epilogue complete its plausible aftermath. Differentiate deep coalition from shelf warning by what those recipients actually risk/do. The proposed physical recipient with arrival/loss and service credential is a promising specific response, still unplayed/unintegrated in this baseline.

### D2 — Two concealed endings have different titles but the same normally reached story

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence. `endings.gd:3,5` defines distinct original Silent Exodus and Hidden Harbour stories. Yet `endings.gd:17–21` overrides both with the identical living-witness outcome/story whenever witness_arrival is quietus/drone_a/drone_b. `witness_evacuation.gd:121–128` writes that fact on normal landed recovery, and `resolution_missions.gd:21` requires landing. Thus this is the ordinary completed concealed path, not an exceptional fallback. Only carrier and rescue/kit log vary; the text no longer references selected deep/shelf thread.

**Player consequence:** Replaying a different first fork to discover a different concealed resolution yields essentially the same rescue/account after many repeated operations. Four labels overstate actual ending distinctness.

**Release severity / class:** High against a multiple-ending campaign promise; **Expansion**. Not a proposal to revert the earned single-witness rescue to unsupported population-wide guarantees.

**Expected quality:** A distinct ending should complete the different route's central stake and show a meaningful reason the earlier branch mattered.

**Substantive improvement:** Preserve the physically landed survivor and recovery particulars, but make their account/harbour consequence resolve the deep evacuation question versus the shelf inhabited-shelter question differently. The distinction can be a short scene plus an evidenced route-specific consequence, without inventing more branches.

### D3 — Earlier discoveries/results rarely become later playable consequences

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence, qualitative consequence unplayed. Real first-fork differences are preserved: distinct7/8,9/10 and hardware. But `campaign_consequences.gd:29–30` persists Warning Chain's local receipt versus mast-only result; `context:39–40` supplies facts to later missions; `resolution_missions.gd:37–77` does not use that result to shape12/13. `confluence_region.gd:5–8,29` mainly consumes deep/shelf as spawn/briefing variation; common geometry, sources, guards and objectives otherwise remain. Listening Well comparison is consumed by `archive_recap.gd:10–11` and retention, without later mission response. Fixed `endings.gd:8–15` does not use these discoveries, resource history or warning proof.

**Player consequence:** A player can perform a more careful experiment or verify service delivery and receive a retained fact, but most later decisions remain unchanged. The fiction promises that returning with better knowledge matters more than retrieving checklist items; the campaign often rewards the completion flag and route alone.

**Release severity / class:** High depth/agency weakness; **Expansion**. Existing divergent middle operations and equipment are substantive, so “all choices cosmetic” would be false.

**Expected quality:** A modest branching campaign can converge, but prior knowledge should alter some later plan, available cooperation, trust, danger, or intelligible consequence.

**Substantive improvement:** Choose a few existing, meaningful facts rather than proliferating flags. Carry verified warning delivery, optional Quietus signature knowledge or tender participation into an altered encounter affordance/recipient response. Make the player recognize the payoff through behavior. Current ID13 proposal may improve the service-credential case; other facts remain gaps.

### D4 — Fleet growth ends early, leaving limited new build decisions in the late campaign

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence. All four general upgrade unlocks are tied only to5,7,8 in `craft_upgrades.gd:5–12`; correlator/sampler also7/8 (`campaign_consequences.gd:23–28`). Equipment is real, with effective slot opportunity costs (`equipment_catalog.gd:5–21`). Later9/10/11/12/13 introduce no additional growth. `fleet_inventory.gd:6–9` removes any module ever recorded lost, and `recover:21–32` commits only first successful recovery. No reacquisition route appears in the inspected campaign/equipment consumers. Meanwhile each `simulation.gd:79–90` start restores bare drone bodies, hull, air and all tool supplies. Failure/retry/replay never spends stock by explicit inventory contract.

**Player consequence:** Builds become static just when campaign sophistication should increase. A costly successful extraction can permanently remove an interesting specialist option, leaving fewer future decisions; there is no further authored “recover, rebuild or change specialization” arc. Conversely repeated failed tool-heavy attempts carry no supply consequence, so this is not a sustained fleet scarcity game. These last two observations describe the actual design, not independently proven balance defects: irreversible equipment loss is an intentional stakes choice, and failure protection preserves finishability. No player evidence yet establishes harmful equipment starvation or a need to replenish stock.

**Release severity / class:** High against long-game growth ambition; **Expansion**. Meaningful module recovery and slot tradeoffs are genuine gains, not bugs to remove. Permanent loss itself is a valid design choice; the weakness is its sparse surrounding progression.

**Expected quality:** Persistent progression should sustain several meaningful changes of plan through the campaign, with understandable rewards and a viable response to setbacks. A currency/roguelike economy is not mandatory.

**Substantive improvement:** Develop a coherent limited set of later capability choices and at least one authored response to loss: recover a replacement through a risky optional opportunity, exchange a capability, or unlock a compensating alternative. Tune it with actual fit/bare comparisons; avoid adding stat tiers that leave play unchanged.

### D5 — Post-tutorial revelations repeat the answer instead of deepening the investigation; the rescued witness never testifies

**Evidence / confidence:** High source content confidence; emotional judgment unplayed. Tutorial3 already says no weapons/departure (`missions.gd:59–62`); tutorial5 decodes fracture/evacuation (`65–71`). Deep7 confirms evacuation, Wake9 confirms evacuation line, Confluence11 again says casting line makes departing hulls and joins the fracture warning (`divergence_missions.gd:20–26`; `onward_missions.gd:14–17`; `confluence_region.gd:23,30`). Shelf8 and10 mainly forward the known warning. Witness12's mechanics are real, but its site evidence, recovered record, saved facts and ending describe an occupied pod, delivery craft and losses; no individual testimony, motive, contested information or distinctive person is actually supplied (`resolution_missions.gd:50,56`; `witness_evacuation.gd:88–90,121–128`; `endings.gd:21`).

**Player consequence:** The second arc often feels like transporting further confirmations of the tutorial's answer. Rescuing a “living witness” changes the cargo type without giving the player someone or something specific to care about. The unresolved Listening Well imitation does not mature into a discovery.

**Release severity / class:** High narrative/pacing weakness; **Expansion**. Short atmospheric prose can carry this game's story; a dialogue system, named cast or certainty about alien identity is not required.

**Expected quality:** Each substantial operation should change the player's understanding, complicate a previous assumption, or personally invest them in an outcome. The ambiguity can concern intent rather than basic task instructions.

**Substantive improvement:** Author an actual new revelation or conflict for each post-tutorial leg, with operational expression. Let the witness contribute one concrete, route-sensitive account that changes what concealment/public warning means. Preserve alien strangeness; do not substitute additional generic evacuation logs.

### D6 — Late enemy encounters rely on the same independent beam problem, with less coordination than the tutorial finale

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence; difficulty/feel requires play. `simulation.gd:980–1011` gives all roles identical visual acquisition, target-first scan, four-second beam and drone instant kill/hull drain. Patrollers stop their route on sight; sentinels/hunters path toward target, with sentinels tether/return and hunters higher speed (`1003–1005,1029–1031,1053–1086`). These are real role differences. All investigate the strongest local arrival and search a last-known area. Only mission5 sets `contact_report_revision` (`missions.gd:121–123`), and `contact_reports.gd:10,52` explicitly limits peer reports to5. Mixed late rosters in7–13 receive no equivalent collaboration or new behavior.

**Player consequence:** The new roster labels/regions do not necessarily generate new enemy problems. Once “break sight or EMP for12s” is understood, much escalation is positioning/speed/density. The information-carrying climax can feel less organizationally sophisticated than the tutorial finale.

**Release severity / class:** High encounter-depth weakness over a long campaign; **Expansion**. This is not proof all encounters are easy: genuine Foundry drone loss and Confluence hull death contradict that. No demand for new weapons, omniscient hunts or arbitrary reactive difficulty.

**Expected quality:** A small roster can support a great stealth campaign if enemies produce learnable, differentiated plans and authored combinations develop those plans. Superior technology should feel intelligent as well as lethal.

**Substantive improvement:** Develop bounded cooperation/role interaction in selected late encounters: guard handoff, evidence-backed investigation cover, a hunter reacting to another guard's stale report, or protecting moving industrial activity. Preserve fair knowledge and clear counters. Prefer fewer deep authored situations over adding names/weapons.

### D7 — The pressure instrument names global hunt stages that do not describe corresponding enemy behavior

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence. `recon_consequences.gd:6–13,25–34,49–57` computes a global own-operation hunt/floor/stage from actions/time/record count, labeling thresholds DORMANT/STIRRING/SEARCHING/HUNTING/LOCKDOWN. Enemy sensing instead uses each guard's locally earned alert_floor/readiness (`14–24`; `simulation.gd:984,991,1002,1031`). The global hunt value does not feed enemy behavior. This separation deliberately protects fair information and is not a missing omniscience “fix.” Genuine cautious critic reports difficulty understanding which action caused INVESTIGATE/pressure. Genuine stakes player saw OP PRESSURE DORMANT on a route shortly before a hunter killed Quietus; they also explicitly missed the actual visible later beam warning, so pressure is not established as the cause of death.

**Player consequence:** A tactical stage name can sound like the enemy's actual current state while it is only an abstract action estimate. The player may optimize a meter rather than learn local acoustic causality; late pressure does not acquire a coherent campaign arc just because the label escalates.

**Release severity / class:** Medium/high comprehension/system-design weakness; **Expansion**, not mere label polishing. Existing instructions defer routine caveats/labels; a substantive solution must connect decisions and received consequences.

**Expected quality:** A stealth game's risk instrument should help predict a useful consequence without exposing hidden knowledge. Distinct local threats and overall action burden can coexist, but the instrument should teach that distinction through causal feedback.

**Substantive improvement:** Show the operator's emitted action and the legitimately observed resulting investigation/return behavior in one coherent tactical feedback chain. Give persistent local readiness an operationally meaningful and discoverable role in extraction. Do not simply rename five stages or globally buff every unseen Siren.

### D8 — Photograph quality is an opaque score on unchanged evidence, with no intelligence consequence

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence plus genuine first exposure. `recon_consequences.gd:35–48` computes photo quality from distance and craft: drone base.55 + proximity up to.45, Quietus base.25 + same proximity. Thus even a perfect zero-distance Quietus image tops out at.70/B; A requires average≥.95. `missions.gd` evidence text and success gates do not vary with quality; all grades preserve endings by specification. `camera_feed.gd:46–52` displays the same fixed illustration independent of actual viewpoint; `evidence_catalog.gd:2–9` has seven images. Genuine cautious player learned29%/C only in debrief, then approached closer and got87%/B, without knowing formula/craft contribution. Later source may present grade data in archives; no ordinary proof prospective explanation succeeds.

**Player consequence:** Risking a closer approach yields a better number rather than more useful knowledge. A player fulfilling the Quietus fallback can be surprised by an unattainable top grade. This weakens photography as a signature act of investigation.

**Release severity / class:** Medium depth/reward clarity weakness; **Expansion** for useful observations/viewpoints; grade display alone would be **Later polish**. Fixed illustrations are explicitly accepted Pass0 placeholders, not a PR blocker.

**Expected quality:** The player should understand why a risky closer/alternate observation is valuable before spending exposure. Useful evidence variation can be optional and must preserve fallback finishability.

**Substantive improvement:** Make framing/proximity reveal a tactically or narratively useful detail, or make grade an honest optional mastery challenge with a clear prospective preview and craft rationale. Do not gate essential branch completion behind A grades.

### D9 — Ending exploration requires deleting the campaign and repeating its full introduction

**Evidence / confidence:** High source confidence. `expedition_campaign.gd:7–15` gates the fork behind all0–5 and unlocks only the first selected route. First history/Confluence choice is intentionally preserved (`campaign_consequences.gd:41–46,37`). `main.gd:547–558` new campaign explicitly deletes completed/unlocked/expedition/evidence/photo_records/fleet/fittings; no retained ending collection. A route has eleven operation completions, so four ending campaigns require44 completions including24 repeats of the six introductory operations, absent retries. This is structural arithmetic, not a human time estimate.

**Player consequence:** The player has to destroy their evidence collection/growth and replay familiar opening lessons to compare branch consequences. With normally identical concealed stories, the reward is especially weak. Fixed sources/layout/patrol starts also limit novelty on repeated campaigns.

**Release severity / class:** High for a branch/replay-focused campaign; **Expansion**. Fresh replay is expressly accepted Pass0 and honest, not current incompatibility or unauthorized reset behavior.

**Expected quality:** Replay should respect learned competence and preserve meaningful accomplishments. A campaign can commit decisions without making all alternate-route exploration start from zero.

**Substantive improvement:** Preserve ending/evidence achievements separately from the active committed history and offer deliberate chapter/fork replay or independent campaigns when breadth warrants it. Keep ordinary first-run commitment intact; avoid silently rewriting history. Add authored replay uncertainty only if it creates new reasoning rather than random unfairness.

### D10 — Signature environmental systems are isolated features rather than a campaign language

**Evidence / confidence:** High source deployment confidence. Search of all operation definitions finds currents only in `confluence_region.gd:22`, earned_terrain only for11 in `missions.gd:20–25`, and thermal_fronts only3 in `missions.gd:85–88`. The thermal validator itself accepts only3 (`thermal_front.gd:6–14`). Other charts intentionally remain precharted (`V7_TO_GODOT_PARITY.md:9`). Acoustic diffraction is real across fresh missions (`acoustic_paths.gd:41–85`) but no reflections or finite sound travel (`5`, parity14). Gates in10/11/12 genuinely alter navigation and sound.

**Player consequence:** Learning to reason with a cold front does not pay forward; first-face unknown terrain/current work arrives suddenly late, then disappears. The ocean often operates as static corridor cover rather than a coherent space whose physical rules the campaign develops.

**Release severity / class:** Medium/high campaign-depth weakness; **Expansion**. Different environments should not all contain every system, and adding currents everywhere would not solve it. Reflections are explicitly pending and not automatically higher value than encounter design.

**Expected quality:** Repeatedly teach, test and recombine a few signature physical affordances so the player's acquired understanding matters. Environmental complexity should change a plan, not merely increase travel distance.

**Substantive improvement:** Extend proven mechanics into a small authored progression: an early readable assist/opposition current, later simultaneous stationkeeping/listening choice, a thermal route that costs information, a downstream combined withdrawal. Validate changed routes/tool expenditure through genuine play.

### D11 — The introduction does not yet reliably teach the costs the later game expects players to reason about

**Evidence / confidence:** Genuine first-exposure cautious report on `e3a541fb24c2a3f136121150b7a0d2960546fda7`, plus current source. Report path below. In0 the player saw no enemy and used no tools, then learned bare drones at their initial position counted OUTSIDE RECOVERY. They learned photo grade only after exposure. In1 they successfully used finite countermeasures under named beam warnings and physically recovered drones, showing learnability, not universal failure. Current `main.gd:454–460` still primarily teaches by prose; `simulation.gd:87` gives every finite tool immediately. `module_recovery.gd:81–92` departure confirmation concerns actual modules; bare craft have no equivalent consequential module warning, consistent with free bare replacement. Source/played revision distinction matters: this critic did not establish every exact current presentation detail.

**Player consequence:** The player can leave the tutorial believing a required success photo is a good photo and “bring Quietus home” fully describes recovery.
