## 2. Topic roll-up for the June build comparison

Cites are file and line from the entries in section 3 (full cites inside each entry).

### Stationary-sub model
- No stationary-sub model in this slice. The repo submarine (Quietus) and two drones move continuously under route orders. Own noise is a continuous function of speed: radius = 430 * (speed/78)^2, so 0 at rest, 430 m at cruise 78 (tests/acoustic_path_tests.gd L11-12, acoustic_footprint_tests.gd L13). Idle is explicitly "never safe" (acoustic_footprint_tests L11).
- Speeds: PATROL 25, ALERT 48 (hunter 64 in the priced-shots mirror table), Quietus silent 42 / cruise 78, drone quiet 42 / transit 96 (route_program.gd L55-57; photo_pricing_table.gd L21-22; rule_table values from earlier entries).
- Settled thresholds repeat everywhere: below 2 m/s for local work (winch, transfer, gates), below 12 m/s for recovery mouth, decode, and extraction. Work timers reset to 0 if the craft moves (blackwater_delivery L179, module_recovery L89).

### Beam lock
- Lock accumulates on a visual target; lock above 3 s counts as "acquired" in tests (tests/attention_scenarios.gd L15-16); the first-siren plan names a "four-second lock" (earlier entry). A target change needs a fresh telegraph: lock drops below 0.5 within 0.3 s of switching target (attention_scenarios L17-19).
- A single 1.0 s threat tick at 100 m gives lock==1.0 and leaves hull at 100, "full telegraph" for patroller, sentinel, hunter (acoustic_hearing_tests.gd L41-42). Hearing alone never grants lock: lock 0, target -1, no contact (L21).
- Locking Siren holds emitter standoff, more than 110 m after 4.5 s (attention_scenarios L27-29).
- Sweeper "winds up before its beam; the warning gives a bearing and a count" (photo_findings.gd L17). Onboarding warning fires on fresh contact (age < 0.3 s) in mode LOCKING or BEAM with lock > 0 and sight; it pauses time once (onboarding_hints.gd L44-53, L80-82).
- Interceptor and Warden use windup then burst/pressure, not a beam: windup 2.0 s (interceptor) / 2.5 s (warden), engagement 500 m, burst 200 m/s for 2 s, hit hull -20 or drone battery -30, pressure patch radius 85 m for 6 s (industrial_threats.gd L52,L76-90,L78,L101-102). Threat tick sets e.lock=0 and e.target=-1 each tick for these roles (L24).

### Stun display
- Stun is a numeric field on the Siren dict (stun 0.0) and shows as mode strings, not a dedicated overlay: "DISRUPTED", or "PINNED" when pin_time > 0 (industrial_threats.gd L27-28). Disruption resets interceptor/warden windup phase and patches.
- Stunned or disrupted listener cannot lock or beam (acoustic_hearing_tests L43-44). Glass Reach machinery stuns buoys 120 s and clears their report packet (glass_reach_work L89); effect.quiet stuns buoy/relay for 90 s default (L126).
- Tool help text: "DISRUPT: briefly interrupt nearby Sirens" (tool_help.gd L6); onboarding says disruption lasts 12 s and torpedo pins one Siren 8 s (earlier entry onboarding_text.gd L41).

### Audio and touch handling
- Audio: all cues are PCM from Soundscape (ambience, ping, camera, emp, record, loss, extraction, alert, engine, creak, beam, factory, salvage, plus recon, decoy), peak below 0.95 (tests/audio_scenarios.gd L5-9). Engine gain/pitch table: silent 42 gives .07 / 1.17, noisy 78 gives .35 / 1.53, rest .0001 / .75, counterthrust at zero ground speed .04 / .99 (L34-44). Mute silences both buses (L46). Master/music/effects sliders in settings, haptics only on Android (frontend_settings.gd L13-16, L19-23). Challenge session suspends campaign audio and resumes on return (challenge_session.gd L21-24,L102). Earlier entries: tension from fresh recon threat only, attack 0.7 s, release 4 s, five effect voices max.
- Contradiction to check: audio/ATTRIBUTION says recordings, not synthesized, while ATTRIBUTION.md L9 says "authored synthesis" (earlier entries F009-F010); audio_scenarios calls Soundscape.samples/stream (Soundscape file not in slice).
- Touch: 48 dp minimum target everywhere (frontend.gd L70-72; torpedo_aim.gd L5; all_stop_ui_tests L30). Map and camera ignore InputEvent.DEVICE_ID_EMULATION mouse events so a touch is not processed twice (route_program_preview.gd L66; earlier camera_feed.gd L61-62). The ALL STOP UI test fires touch plus emulated mouse pairs at 2520x1080 and 2184x1968 to prove it (all_stop_ui_tests L13-21). Pick radii scale max(touch*0.32 to 0.5, N*world_scale). Order guard: replace-plan taps set a pending marker, undo window 10 s (order_guard.gd L6,L38-55). Long-press help 0.5 s (tool_help L3).

### Recon LOS
- RECON/Survey radius 560 m for eight seconds from a fixed survey_origin, with NO line-of-sight test: survey_visible is a pure distance check (world_region.gd L5,L55-58); chart observe() certifies every cell and every face in the disk "including occluded faces" (terrain_chart.gd L57-65). Text confirms "including behind cover" (earlier split_channel_region L23).
- Passive receivers and active ping DO need LOS: observe() requires _seen_rect for clear cells (terrain_chart L52-55); active ping reach 650 m needs line_clear (world_region L46; rendezvous_transport L40).
- Camera recon: sight 170 m (x0.65 in darkness), within fov/2 + 0.3 rad, Depth.clear required (photo_capture.gd L358-363). Siren photo candidate needs fresh contact (<= 0.6 s) and contact_visible (L397).
- Siren sight: 290 m, 180 m against a silent craft, scaled by Recon.readiness (photo_pricing table L15-16; photo_pricing.gd L216-217).

### Win and lose precedence
- Challenge mode: rule violation sets outcome "lost" unconditionally after the sim tick, so lose beats win (earlier challenge_run.gd L41-45); result.loss true if abandoned, not won, or violated (L57). Results screen prefers sim.rule_failure text (challenge_session.gd L89).
- Campaign: outcome strings "won", "withdrawn", otherwise "lost" (expedition_operations.gd L22). Loss grants no new fitting, continuation or committed history (L61-66). Failed dives still keep field observations.
- Many validators enforce "won requires X": glass reach material recovered, choir completion, warning plant result, recipient receipt, witness landed, concealed harbour receipt (glass_reach_work valid L146; warning_chain L125; warning_recipient L135; witness_evacuation L165; concealed_harbour L162; blackwater_delivery L89).
- Recipient or load death sets outcome "lost" at once (warning_recipient L88-91; industrial_jobs L140). Arrival receipt is historical: "a later beam can still kill" (warning_recipient L125). Witness/recipient campaign credit also needs Quietus recovery (witness_evacuation_view L104; warning_recipient_view L117).
- Extraction requires speed below 12 m/s, band 1, within 65 m of the corridor, clear line (industrial_jobs L164; glass_reach_work L106; module_recovery L102).
